## Artwork handling

Original artwork is stored unchanged in `public/portfolio/`. The site serves
resized WebP display copies from `public/portfolio-optimized/` for faster page
loads, then opens the original file in the full-size viewer. Display copies must
not be recolored, retouched, filtered, or otherwise altered.
